# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling,
deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or retaliation against
someone who reports a concern are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently exclude participants whose behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns through a private channel listed on the primary
maintainer's public GitHub profile. If no private project channel is available,
use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
for safety-related incidents and do not publish sensitive details in an issue.
Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as reasonably possible.
